# GROHNPETREL Apfelschorle – naturtrüb aus Bremen

**GROHNPETREL Apfelschorle** is the alcohol-free line of the Bremen drinks brand GROHNPETREL: cloudy, unfiltered apple juice from North German orchards, mixed with sparkling spring water. It contains 0.0% alcohol and comes in four flavours.

**Shop the flavours:** [GROHNPETREL Apfelschorle](https://business.constructor.university/skuas/#schorle)

## The four GROHNPETREL Apfelschorle flavours

1. **Apfel** – the classic: Boskoop and Elstar apples, 65% juice, crisp and dry
2. **Apfel-Rhabarber** – tart garden rhubarb on a cloudy apple base, 60% juice
3. **Apfel-Ingwer** – apple with cold-pressed ginger root for a warm, spicy finish
4. **Apfel-Beere** – blackberries, raspberries and blackcurrants with Bremen apples

Each flavour is sold in a 330 ml crown-cap glass bottle and a 330 ml aluminium can.

## Why Apfelschorle?

After beer, Apfelschorle is one of Germany's best-loved drinks: half juice, half sparkling water, and refreshing at any time of day. GROHNPETREL brings it home to Bremen, with the St. Petri Dom on every label.
